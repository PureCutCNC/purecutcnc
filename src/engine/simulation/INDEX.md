# INDEX — src/engine/simulation/

Material-removal simulation. Flat, round and square stock all use one radial
heightfield: `topZ` stores one height per cell and renders through a GPU
heightfield mesh. A square blank is the round bar's heightfield whose cells
start at the blank's true cross section; nothing downstream branches on stock
shape. Pure logic + Three.js mesh builders — no React.

## Core
- `aroundWrap.ts` — compile-time Around shader chunks: shared wrap and radial-normal formulas, periodic neighbor lookup and render bounds; `aroundBounds` is centred on the true pivot and defaults its maximum to the grid's radial envelope (`stockTopZ`), so a square blank's corners fit at `pivot ± side/√2` rather than a lifted `0..2·maxRadius` while a round bar stays byte-identical. The neighbour lookup is a preprocessor-free integer chunk (`AROUND_CELL_INDEX_GLSL`) so the scalar test support can run the GPU's own code; flat materials retain their original shaders
- `types.ts` — `SimulationGrid`, `DirtyRegion`, `SimulationBuildOptions`, `SimulationStats`; `stockTopZ` is the grid's enclosing radial maximum (the bar radius, or a square blank's corner radius) and `initialTopZ` is an optional immutable per-cell starting baseline used only by removal statistics. `SquareBarDescriptor` and `FlatRoundBarDescriptor` are initialization input; the grid carries no runtime stock-shape discriminator (the flat profile's `length` is what the static lower-half mesh spans)
- `grid.ts` — grid spec resolution and `createSimulationGrid` allocation. A square blank enters only here: `validateSquareBar` checks the descriptor, `seedSquareTopZ` fills each across cell with its starting radius `halfSide / max(|sin θ|, |cos θ|)` (constant along the bar), and `createSimulationGrid` snapshots the result as `initialTopZ`. `stockTopZ` is the corner envelope (`side/√2`); the true pivot stays `frame.radius`. A round bar on a flat Top/Bottom setup enters here too (issue #1049): `validateFlatRoundBar` checks its profile, `seedFlatRoundBarTopZ` fills each across cell with `pivot + sqrt(radius² − d²)` and starts cells with `|d| ≥ radius` at the grid's empty value, and the result is snapshotted as `initialTopZ`. It is a top-down heightfield, so only the bar's upper half exists; the descriptor is rejected together with `squareBar` or `wrap`
- `tools.ts` — `cutterSurfaceZ`: the cutter's lower-surface profile used to lower flat-grid cells; `cutterRayInterval` (and its allocation-free `cutterRayIntervalInPlace`) is the wrapped counterpart: the exact radial span the cutter's solid removes on one ray, solved in closed form for the flat cylinder, the ball nose plus shank, and the V-bit cone plus shank
- `replay.ts` — the one cutting path for every stock shape. A flat grid and the plasma torch keep the flat-sheet kernel byte-for-byte (a torch cuts a flat one-kerf, full-depth slot along every torch-on move and a kerf-wide hole at each pierce). A wrapped (Around) grid dispatches to a second kernel that resolves each cell against the cutter's true solid (issue #1016 C2-F8): the unrolled across distance is arc length at the frame radius `R0 = period/2π`, so a cell at radius `r` and angle `D` sits `r·sin D` across the cutter and `r·cos D` along it, not flat. The cell keeps one radius, so only a cutter that reaches the outside lowers it; one that passes through the ray's middle leaves the outer shell. Its across bounding box uses the tight profile-specific reach (flat `R0·atan(rt/tip)`, ball `R0·asin(rt/(tip+rt))`, V-bit at its truncation) and cells at or below the move's deepest tip are skipped before the trig; every cell in the applied piece's footprint is resolved against all periodic images and their ray intervals are iterated to the order-independent fixed point (F8b), so overlapping seam copies cannot make finished playback differ from a batch replay and a cell the cutter already contains is removed in that frame. Statistics compare each cell with its own `initialTopZ` baseline when one exists (removed count, minimum top and maximum removed depth) and keep the uniform `stockTopZ` comparison for flat/round; the cell loop is allocation-free with per-move cutter dispatch (see `replay.test.ts` for the flat-sheet reference-parity contract)
- `playback.ts` — playback state: poses, options, grid cloning for stepped playback. Forward seeks advance incrementally (cuts are monotonic); the dirty region accumulates until the caller uploads and clears it; `cloneSimulationGrid` copies the mutable `topZ` and shares the immutable `initialTopZ` by reference, and `reset` restores the prior-operation base grid's heights. Each subdivided piece remembers the source move it came from and hands it to `applyMoveToGrid` as the `sweep`; the piece bounds only the elapsed footprint, so a partial application cuts what the cutter has reached while the source move supplies the cut depth, and the fixed point makes repeated pieces idempotent (issue #1016 C2-F8/F8b)

## Rendering (Three.js)
- `gpuMesh.ts` — builds a `DataTexture` over the grid's `topZ` array and an instanced row-strip surface with one flat top per cell (no shared corners that can collapse narrow tab bridges); bounds cover the shader-displaced Y range through `aroundBounds` and the grid's radial envelope, so a square blank's corners are not clipped; `uploadHeightfieldRegion` pushes only the dirty rectangle to the GPU via `texSubImage2D`
- `instancedBoundary.ts` — boundary walls + stock underside as instanced row-strips whose geometry the vertex shader derives from `gl_InstanceID` + the heightfield texture (GLSL3 `texelFetch`); O(cols) template memory at any detail, no CPU rebuilds while cutting. The one heightfield boundary path for flat, round and square static and playback views, with the same radial-envelope bounds. A step gets a wall lit by its own normal; a riser inside a slope is drawn too, but lit as the surface it belongs to. `uWallFloorZ` is the lowest wall height — the stock bottom by default, the equator for a flat round bar, whose static lower half draws below it (issue #1049)
- `flatRoundBarLowerHalf.ts` — the fixed lower half of a flat round bar (issue #1049): `createFlatRoundBarLowerHalf` builds a half-cylinder of the descriptor's `radius`/`length` about its pivot and centre, closed by half-disc end caps, from the same descriptor that seeds the grid. It shares the heightfield's colour, light rig and depth darkening, spans the lower semicircle only, and returns `null` for a block, square blank or Around setup, so every other scene is unchanged. Render-only: nothing cuts it, and it is created/disposed with the grid in both static and playback views
- `heightfieldShader.ts` — the heightfield surface shader material (`createHeightfieldMaterial`) plus the GLSL both meshes share: the `LIGHTING_GLSL` light rig, the `STEP_GLSL` step/slope predicate (`edgeIsStep`), the smoothed surface normal (`SLOPE_GRADIENT_GLSL` + `SURFACE_NORMAL_GLSL`), and `DEPTH_DARKEN_GLSL` (shared with the static lower half). The surface takes a lighting gradient only across an edge `edgeIsStep` calls a slope, interpolated across the cell so it is continuous over cell borders and widened when a cell is under a pixel; `instancedBoundary.ts` lights the steps it leaves flat, so the two meshes cannot disagree about which of them shades an edge
- `toolMesh.ts` — builds/disposes the moving cutter mesh group; a plasma torch gets the same plain flat-endmill cylinder as a flat endmill

## Tests
- `aroundReplay.test.ts` — multi-turn folded replay, periodic cutter overlap on both seam sides and axes, dirty-region union, flat-reference parity and playback/scrub parity
- `aroundWrap.test.ts` — executes the actual scalar GLSL against the frozen rotary transforms on both axes in every quadrant; tests bar-group turn/tool placement, radial normals, cylinder bounds, the square corner envelope about the true pivot and compile-time material variants; also runs the seam's integer `aroundCell` lookup (both bar axes, along index never wrapped) and the `sameAroundFrame` comparison that keys the viewport's effects (issue #395 G3)
- `flatRoundBar.test.ts` — a round bar on a flat Top/Bottom setup (issue #1049): the seeded grid matches the analytic `pivot + sqrt(radius² − d²)` profile cell by cell on both bar axes, both units and both faces, cells outside `|d| ≥ radius` start empty, a block/square/Around grid keeps its exact output, a pocket lowers only cells the cutter reaches, and the descriptor is validated
- `flatRoundBarLowerHalf.test.ts` — the static lower half a flat round bar's scene must contain (issue #1049 follow-up): a half-cylinder of the bar's own radius and length, at or below the equator, on both axes, both units and both faces; a block or square scene builds none; and the boundary walls' floor is the equator for the round bar and the stock bottom for a block
- `gpuMesh.test.ts` — heightfield texture/mesh construction and per-cell top geometry
- `tabbedEdgeSimulation.test.ts` — real rectangle/Edge Out/auto-tab toolpath replay at two detail levels, including playback and through-cut neighbors
- `instancedBoundary.test.ts` — strip template scaling, instance counts, group wiring, and the step predicate and surface normal the surface and walls share
- `heightfieldShader.test.ts` — the step/slope predicate and the lighting gradient's scalar helpers, run from the GLSL source: a ball scallop at ~3 cells per pass is a slope, tab bridges and cut-through rims stay steps, a step at or below half a cell is a slope, the gradient is continuous across cell borders and takes nothing from a step. The texture lookups and wiring around those helpers are restated in the test; the assembled shaders are covered by the rendered checks in `e2e/viewportViews.smoke.spec.ts`
- `glslScalar.testSupport.ts` — test support that compiles the shaders' scalar GLSL functions to JavaScript, so those tests call the text the GPU compiles rather than a TypeScript copy. `ivec2` is a plain `{ x, y }` and `int`/`ceil` are builtins, with function-read uniforms passed as `globals`, so integer code like `aroundCell` runs too (issue #395 G3)
- `replay.test.ts` — optimized flat cut-kernel parity against the `cutterSurfaceZ` reference; the wrapped seam as a pure one-period translation (the old flat-sheet parity moved on purpose with F8) and the guarantee that flat/round grids gain no square keys
- `playback.test.ts` — playback move preparation, feed scaling, seek parity and dirty-region accumulation; plus the round clone key contract and wrapped round playback-versus-batch parity
- `wrappedCutterGeometry.test.ts` — the wrapped cutter's true width (issues #1016 C2-F8/F8b): random moves on round and square grids, both bar axes and all three profiles, checked cell by cell against a dense physical oracle that tests points against the cutter's real solid; a ray through the cutter's middle keeps the outer shell; the ball shank carries a ray the nose cannot; the 25 mm square-to-round case lowers zero retained-end cells; a round-bar groove matches the oracle through its walls; seam copies, playback batch == subdivided and reset; the reviewer's overlapping-seam move finishes playback exactly equal to batch under default, fine and unsplit pieces; a partial playback cuts the cell at (5, 0) at X = 4.5 while leaving a cell beyond the elapsed footprint alone; and the 25 mm ball replay stays inside its CPU-time bound at detail 280 and 1120 (the full flat reach mutation trips the 1120 bound)
- `squareGrid.test.ts` — square grid initialization: the true cross section seeded at every across cell, the immutable baseline, the corner render envelope with the pivot at side/2, virgin zero stats, old-grid key identity and descriptor validation
- `squareReplay.test.ts` — the square blank through the shared kernel and playback: corner cells above the inscribed radius lower monotonically and never rise, a tool above the envelope removes nothing, angle-changing moves follow round behavior, a wrapped square grid uses the round seam copies, and batch/playback/subdivision/seek/reset agree. Statistics measure each cell's own baseline (a virgin square reports zero removed), and the actual generated rough keeps untouched ends square while the actual finish replays and seeks
