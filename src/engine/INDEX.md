# INDEX — src/engine/

Pure-logic CAM core. No React, no DOM. Everything here is testable in isolation. New features here **must** have unit tests.

## Top-level files
- `clipperOpenPaths.ts` — typed seam for clipper-lib's open-path API (`addOpenSubject`, `openPathsFromPolyTree`); confines the casts the local clipper-lib typings force
- `clipperOpenPaths.test.ts` — tests for the above
- `csg.ts` — manifold-3d CSG wrappers, STL transformed-geometry cache. Feature input is gated through `store/helpers/featureRoles.modelFeatures()` — construction geometry never reaches the model (issue #199). Round stock (issue #392) draws as a cylinder on its centreline, as do a chuck/tailstock on a bar; an axis-aligned block and every other fixture keep the extruded box. Around features are wrapped onto the bar itself (issue #395): a visible Around subtract starts from the bar cylinder (`roundBarSolid`) and cuts, an Around add is `union(current, intersection(slab, bar))`, and an Around region gets a wrapped display-only slab. In a **rotary project** the bar itself is the model on every face (#1054 slice 1): `buildBarModelSolid` seeds from `barStockSolid` — `roundBarSolid` or the square blank's `squareBarSolid` prism about the true pivot — so Top and Bottom subtracts cut that bar directly, flat and Around adds are clipped to it, and the Around slabs apply to the same solid, giving one model for all faces. A block project (milling or plasma) builds its flat solid exactly as before; `squareBarSolid` is the square equivalent of `roundBarSolid`
- `csgAroundFace.test.ts` — Around features in the 3D model (issue #395 slice G1): the Around pocket changes a flat scene and leaves vertices on its floor, an Around subtract alone is the bar minus its sector on both bar axes, an Around add alone leaves the uncut bar (#1054 makes the bar the model, so it restores nothing), subtract-then-add keeps the island while add-then-subtract does not, an invisible Around feature leaves the scene byte-identical, and an Around line wraps more than one turn at `z_top − R`
- `csgRotaryBarSeed.test.ts` — the bar seeds the model on every face (#1054 slice 1): a Top, a Bottom and an Around pocket on one rotary bar produce one solid whose volume is the bar's minus the three independently measured pocket volumes and whose bounds are the bar's, round and square on both axes; the square prism is length × side² about the true pivot; block milling and plasma CSG deep-equals the committed base fixture; and a block with only a subtract still builds nothing
- `csgRoundStock.test.ts` — round stock in the scene (issue #392 slice D): the bar's cylinder matches the block's box and every lateral vertex is one radius from the centreline, a chuck/tailstock on a bar is a cylinder about the axis, and a block stock, a step clamp on a bar and a chuck with no stock all keep today's box, position for position
- `constructionExclusion.test.ts` — guard test: construction geometry can never become a machining target, region mask, or CSG input; fails the build if the exclusion regresses
- `profilePolyline.ts` — shared, acyclic profile-to-polyline flattening and contour-closing helpers used by CSG and batched 3D Line overlays
- `aroundFrame.ts` — the Around sheet (issue #395): unroll a round bar into a transient flat block, wrap toolpath distance into unbounded rotary angles, split ramping turns and remap collisions; unwrap for later simulation and reports. A chuck/tailstock band covers every turn the job can reach, and `flatFaceProject` drops the Around features from a flat-face read (returning the input object when there are none). A square blank (#1016 C2-S3) unrolls through the same frame with its **nominal reference cylinder** — radius = side / 2 (the inscribed radius), circumference = π · side — which is a coordinate mapping for the angle a sheet column stands for, not a claim about the blank's surfaces; the real flats and corners stay the sampler's business and the corner radius never enters the sheet. `aroundFrameForOperation` accepts either bar axis-matched (a block is still refused); whether a square blank may be *authored* on the Around face is the store/UI guard's decision, still refusing
- `aroundFrame.test.ts` — off-origin X/mm and Y/inch frames, rotary-sign agreement, transient project contents, split paths and four-strategy parity through the real generation seam; the square blank's sheet is the inscribed radius and π · side and is identical to the round bar of the same box (#1016 C2-S3); mutation checks listed in its header
- `aroundReview.test.ts` — the #395 review fixes at the real generation seam (slice H1): a fixture band across every reachable turn, the `aroundCutReachesCentreline` refusal for a cut the operation worked out itself, and a Top pocket unchanged by an overlapping Around feature; mutation checks listed in its header
- `aroundFold.ts` — folds unwrapped simulation moves at circumference seams, preserving motion metadata and collision indices and stamping every folded point with the program's unreduced rotary angle `a` (issue #395 G2)
- `aroundFold.test.ts` — positive/negative multi-turn folds, both bar axes, exact seam endpoints, metadata, bounds, collision mapping and the unreduced angle (0..540 for a 1.5-turn line, equal on both sides of every seam)
- `aroundFeatureSolid.ts` — the solid one Around feature stands for (issue #395 slice G1): its closed profile swept from its bottom radius to its top radius, cut into quarter-turn bands with a hair of overlap so Manifold can weld them, refined to half the 5° chord budget, then warped onto the bar with the frozen wrap. `null` for an imported model, a line, an open profile or a span inside the centreline; a top at the surface is pushed 2 % past it so a subtract breaks through. Shared by the model, the region mesh and the tests
- `aroundFeatureSolid.test.ts` — the wrapped slab in numbers on both bar axes: radii and mean angle at a quarter turn, volume against the annular sector, no mesh edge over 5° of turn, a seam-crossing shape as one connected solid, one turn further identical, 1.5 turns equal to one ring, and the null cases; mutation checks listed in its header
- `rotaryFrame.ts` — the frozen rotary sign convention (issue #393): `stockSpacePoint` maps a toolpath point `{x, y, z, a}` to the point of the unturned stock under the tool tip, and `toolSpacePoint` is its exact inverse. With the tool above the bar's centreline at radius `r`, the stock point is `r·sin a` across the bar and `r·cos a` up, on either project axis. The map is a rigid rotation about the bar's **true turning pivot** (`barCentre` across, `barPivotZ = thickness / 2` up), so a round bar and a square blank of the same box share it exactly (#1016 C2-S3): the square's corners are material at a larger radius, never a different pivot and never on the rotation path
- `rotaryPrism.ts` — the generated regular polygon prism inside a round bar (issue #1016 C2, the hex/polygon half): `buildRotaryPolygonPrism(stock, { sides, acrossFlats, alongStart, alongEnd, rotationDeg? })` returns an `ImportedTriangleMesh` in final stock coordinates — a closed, outward-wound prism of `2 · sides` vertices and `4 · sides - 4` triangles, its `bounds` computed from the emitted vertices so the persisted-model load fit is an identity. `acrossFlats` is twice the apothem for every count — for an odd count that is not the across-corners distance — rotation 0 puts one flat up, and finite degrees are reduced modulo 360 before the radians conversion so a whole turn is an identity and a huge finite angle cannot overflow to NaN. Every input is validated with a `RangeError` before any mesh storage is allocated — positive finite diameter, finite stock bounds, sides 3..64, finite rotation, ascending along span inside the bar, and across-corners within the bar — and the mesh that is actually emitted is checked afterwards, before it is returned: every stored coordinate finite, a non-zero axial span, all `sides` ring vertices distinct, and no zero-area triangle, so a finite request whose cross-section collapses under Float32 rounding is refused too. Plain geometry: no generator parameter block is stored
- `rotaryPrism.test.ts` — the prism in closed form on both axes (3/4/6 sides, apothem, circumradius, flat-up, closed oriented topology, outward faces, signed volume = polygon area × length, stock-space placement), non-symmetric rotations (37° and −25°, plus a 90° turn that carries the top flat to the −across side), whole-turn identity and huge finite ± rotations against their normalized remainders, the persisted-model identity through the real `loadSTLTransformedGeometry` seam with the model's actual Z bounds (and the 0..diameter stretch it avoids), every refusal plus the non-representable-Float32, collapsed-span, collapsed-cross-section and zero-area-triangle refusals, a small-but-representable cross-section that still builds, inch-scale mirrors of the geometry, persistence and physically sampled tips on both axes, and real flat/ball cutter tips against the sampler's own rule on a finer grid, with the quantisation allowance derived per angle from the turned edges the footprint reaches plus the reference grid step (not a fitted constant), including the real cutter-width effect where the wider tool reaches over the adjacent faces. Mutation checks are listed in its header
- `rotaryCylinder.ts` — `buildRotaryCylinder` builds the validated closed cylinder used as the ordinary finished-model target for square-to-round (#1016 C2-B). It returns an imported triangle mesh in final stock coordinates with actual emitted bounds, so the ordinary persisted model loader preserves its height and pivot; the physical square blank remains stock, with retained ends supplied by the rotary sampler.
- `rotaryFrame.test.ts` — the convention in numbers on a 20 mm bar on both axes: the landmark angles, a point off the centreline, no-angle passthrough, and the round trip in both directions; the square blank (#1016 C2-S3) turns about the true pivot, is identical to the round bar of the same box, and puts its 45° corners under the tool at the corner radius
- `rotaryPatterns.ts` — the pure Thread/Flutes sheet geometry (issue #1016 C1, canned in #1112 F): `rotaryPatternFrame` reads a round bar's zero line, circumference, radius and **chuck-end** origin/direction (minX on X, maxY on Y — the same end Turn/Polygon measure from), and `buildRotaryPatternLines` draws the open lines from `start`/`length` with `starts × pitch` lead, `C/n` spacing and the physical hand re-derived per bar axis. The band is placed from the chuck end but drawn in ascending stock coordinates, so a Y bar's line direction, toolpath and posted angle stay identical to the pre-canned command's. It imports only types and `barStock`, so canned generators can use it without cycling through the store/text/registry.
- `lineRendering.test.ts` — unit tests for `closeLinePolygonIfNeeded`: closed Line profiles append the first point for independent-segment closing; open profiles are unchanged
- `lineBatcher.ts` — converts every visible open profile and closed operation=line profile (including multi-profile text) into at most two `LineSegments2` draw objects, preserving green/default and blue/Subtract colours without connector segments; an Around one (issue #395) is subdivided at 5° of turn and wrapped onto the bar at `z_top − R`
- `lineBatcher.test.ts` — value/object-level tests for independent segment geometry, colours, closed-solid exclusion, multi-profile text, 2,980-contour batching, and GPU resource disposal
- `importedMesh.ts` — STL/OBJ triangle mesh handling: parsing, axis swaps, post-import 3D orientation (`orientImportedMesh`), silhouette extraction, serialization
- `importedMesh.test.ts` — tests for the above
- `importedModelTransform.ts` — shared strict instance-matrix adapter for imported-model preview, CSG, CAM, and export; `modelDataTurnedOver` / `isModelTurnedOver`, the unserializable mark a Bottom setup's read model puts on a model so the mesh loader turns it over (issue #946); plus the post-import 3D orientation math (`ModelOrientation` → matrix, X→Y→Z order, cache key)
- `importedModelTransform.test.ts` — imported-model affine transform and consumer-alignment regressions
- `modelOrientation.test.ts` — post-import 3D orientation (issue #241): rotation order, exact 90° round-trips, rigid-rotation regression across preview/CSG/CAM, and the orientation cache key
- `setupOrientation.ts` — machining-setup orientation (issue #944), the one module that knows how a setup turns the stock: canonical stock point ↔ setup-local point (`canonicalToSetupPoint` / `setupToCanonicalPoint`) through a `SetupFrame` whose pivot is derived from the stock, the derived face (`setupFace`, including Around by kind), where the shared origin lands in stock space, arc-direction reversal, the operation → setup lookup the export pipeline uses (Around has no rigid frame because its stored points already carry rotary angles), and the face-local depth view of a feature's Z span (`depthFromFace` / `spanFromFaceDepth`). Accepts only 0° and 180°; at 0° the point is returned untouched. The point transforms are generic: a caller's rotary angle (issue #393) passes through unchanged, and a point that had none gains no key
- `setupOrientation.test.ts` — the transforms in numbers on an off-origin, non-square stock: 0° identity, both half turns, the rotary angle carried through, other angles refused, round trips, the stock mapping onto itself, the shared origin on and off the flip centreline, arc direction, and face-local depth keeping floating and partial-depth spans intact. The header lists the mutations each assertion was checked against
- `setupFrameProject.ts` — the project as it sits on the machine for one setup (issue #946). `projectInSetupFrame` turns the whole project into a setup's frame — every feature (plan mirror composed onto its instance transform, Z span reflected through mid-thickness), the stock outline, tabs and clamps; the shared origin is left alone — so the unchanged generators can cut a Bottom operation top-down. `toolpathInStockFrame` carries the result back into stock space (moves, bounds, drill cycles, cut levels). Top returns the same objects, untouched. An imported model is turned over as well: its data is marked with `modelDataTurnedOver` and `loadSTLTransformedGeometry` (`csg.ts`) reflects the mesh inside its already reflected span, so a Bottom setup machines the model's back face
- `setupFrameProject.test.ts` — each piece in numbers on an off-origin stock: vertices, arc sense, circle centres, Z spans (blind, floating, through, named), stock box and outline, tabs, clamps, text glyphs mirrored, an imported model's mesh equal vertex for vertex to the half turn of the mesh as drawn, the plan mirror agreeing **exactly** with the point transform, and a toolpath carried back (moves, re-measured bounds, drill cycles, cut levels, indices)
- `setupTargets.ts` — which features a setup's operations may target (issue #946): same face; or, from the other face, a true through-feature (a subtract reaching both stock faces) or an imported 3D model, both reported as cross-face; else rejected with a reason. `isThroughFeature`, per-target verdicts with depth from the operation's face, `targetAllowedInSetup` (the store's question) and `setupGenerationBlock` (generation's: a wrong-face target produces no motion). `withoutUnreachableSubtracts` applies the same rule to **related** (non-target) features (#1079): a subtract reachable only from the other face is removed, so a Top pocket cannot fold in a Bottom cavity 1.3 below its floor; adds always stay, because they are material on either face. It is applied at generation and at the region entries that bypass generation (`restRegions.ts`'s two draft entries, CAM Plan's analysis project), because those call the resolvers directly; the resolver cannot carry it, since `setupFrameProject` zeroes every setup's turn and a generation-turned project would read as Top. Kept free of the store's target validator because it runs in the toolpath worker. Rotary rough/finish alone may target imported models from Around, and their centreline limits belong to the rotary sampler (#1015). Only a rotary project has Around work at all (issue #1028): outside one, an Around setup or a rotary operation is blocked at generation, and the rotary reach rules answer false. On a **square** blank (#1016 C2-S4) the Around face is rotary-only and model-only — a wrapped 2D path has no constant-depth surface to follow — and the physical-setup guard (`squareRotarySafety.ts`) must admit the workholding and ordinary clamps; `squareRotaryWorkAdmitted` names that one admitted job, every other kind/target/face blocks as `squareRotaryUnsupported` (an error; `setupGenerationBlock` carries the physical guard's `refusal` so a too-small chuck, a placement error or an ordinary-clamp overlap reports `workholdingDiameter` / `workholdingPlacement` / `ordinaryClampOverlap`, and the remaining reasons fall back to `postSetupOperationRefused`, #1016 C2 F3), and `targetAllowedInSetup` refuses **every** target source for a non-rotary square Around kind, a missing `kind` included. Round/flat mismatches keep the historical `aroundStockMismatch` reason and its warning severity.
- `squareRotarySafety.ts` — the physical-setup guard for a square blank on the rotary axis (#1016 C2-S4), one pure verdict asked by generation (`setupTargets.ts`) and again by output (`gcode/motionPipeline.ts`) so the two cannot disagree. A chuck/tailstock that axially overlaps the blank must be centred on the true pivot (`barCentre` across, `barPivotZ = side / 2` up), with its height derived from that pivot and an outer diameter at least the corner envelope `2 · barMaxRadius` — a supported-setup restriction, not a measured bore or a proven jaw collision. An ordinary clamp must not reach the circumscribed cylinder of that envelope: the along span is grown by the cutter radius plus `clampClearanceXY` too, so being past the blank's end face is not clear until the finite cutter body is accounted for, while a clamp with a real gap beyond that is passed. Hidden clamps still hold the bar, a workholding support wholly past the end face (the default tailstock) does not, and every non-finite or non-positive dimension, overflowing far corner, non-finite project clearance, unreadable blank or degenerate axial extent fails closed; `squareRotaryCutterRefusal` additionally refuses an output entry whose emitted cutter is missing, unusable, unsupported, named by another project row (the program's tool number comes from its `id`), labelled in another unit system than the project, differently shaped from the referenced row at the same radius, or whose normalized radius and diameter do not describe one body. It reports the offending clamp and never resizes it or moves the pivot.
- `setupOperationMove.ts` — `planOperationMove` / `applyOperationMove`: moving an operation to another setup re-judges its targets from the destination face, lists the ones it drops, and blocks the move when nothing valid would be left
- `setupReach.ts` — `operationCutRange` (the stock-Z range an operation cuts at a feature, read from its generated toolpath) and `throughFeatureCoverage` (for a through-feature targeted from both faces: meets / gap / unverified). Measured, never inferred: only ranges that were measured and meet are reported as meeting. A rotary operation reports no reach: its path follows the model round the bar, where a single stock-Z range means nothing (#1015).
- `setupReachGeometry.ts` — clips feeding segments to the resolved target contours expanded by a round cutter radius, preserving holes and reserving curve/integer error; interpolates Z only where actual motion intersects the footprint
- `setupReach.test.ts` — multi-target attribution regression from generated Top/Bottom paths, legitimate reach, geometry and unit/instance checks
- `setupTargets.test.ts` — the three modules above: through-feature tolerance, same-face / cross-face / rejected verdicts, the generation block, the Move plan (dropped, kept, blocked), cut ranges with their cutter footprint, and coverage both from hand-built paths and from real generated ones; plus the square blank's Around-only, model-only reach and generation block, and its refusal of every target source (a stock target and a missing `kind` included) for a non-rotary square Around kind (#1016 C2-S4)

## Subfolders
- [toolpaths/](toolpaths/INDEX.md) — toolpath generation (pocket, profile, v-carve, surface rough/finish, drill, edge…). **The heart of CAM.**
- [operations/camPlan/](operations/camPlan/INDEX.md) — deterministic CAM Plan: semantic feature/depth recognition, explainable tool planning, residual-backed rest proposals, dependency ordering, coverage, and shared edge-tab drafts. `operations/autoTabs.ts` is the pure tab-placement builder shared with the manual store action.
- `test-fixtures/` — committed engine-test assets such as `.camj` regression files shared by engine tests, including the issue #401 real cone finish project
- [gcode/](gcode/INDEX.md) — export: post-processing toolpaths into a program in the machine's output dialect (G-code, or ShopBot part files), the shared motion pipeline (machine transform, arc fitting), bundled machine definitions, and the exported-motion debug helpers
- `modelExport/` — model/design export (pluggable format registry: 3D mesh formats and 2D vector formats)
  - `index.ts` — public API and `MODEL_EXPORT_FORMATS` registry
  - `types.ts` — format/option interfaces (`kind: '2d' | '3d'` gates mesh assembly)
  - `assemble.ts` — manifold union → standard Z-up right-handed export mesh
  - `stl.ts` — binary + ASCII STL writers and the `stlExportFormat` entry
  - `svg.ts` — `svgExportFormat`: 2D design SVG at true 1:1, backed by `designPrint/` (issue #257)
- [designPrint/](designPrint/INDEX.md) — vector renderer for the 2D design view: page/scale layout math + SVG/HTML generation for printing (issue #254) and the geometry-only SVG export (issue #257)
- [operationBooklet/](operationBooklet/INDEX.md) — per-operation report model and PDF booklet generation
- [simulation/](simulation/INDEX.md) — heightfield-based material removal sim for flat, round and square stock: one radial grid, replay/stepping, GPU heightfield mesh + shaders
- [nesting/](nesting/INDEX.md) — sheet nesting (#741): pure packer that places copies of part footprints on a sheet with a caller-supplied gap (no-fit polygons over convex pieces, bottom-left fill)

## Conventions
- All public exports flow through each subfolder's `index.ts`.
- Clipper integer-scaling and profile↔path conversion are wrapped in `store/helpers/clipping.ts`; arc/curve reconstruction of Clipper output lives in `toolpaths/arcReconstruction.ts`.
- Coordinates here are **internal** (Y-down). G-code export inverts to Cartesian.
